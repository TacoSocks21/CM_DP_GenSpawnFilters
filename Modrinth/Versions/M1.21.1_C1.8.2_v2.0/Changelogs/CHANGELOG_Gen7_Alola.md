## Updated for Cobblemon 1.8.2
Built for **Cobblemon 1.8.2** (Minecraft 1.21.1). If you're still on Cobblemon 1.7.x, keep using the previous version of this datapack.

### New Pokémon
- Grubbin, Charjabug, Vikavolt
- Rockruff, Lycanroc
- Minior
- Alolan Sandshrew, Alolan Sandslash

### Alpha Pokémon & herds
- Added Cobblemon 1.8's new **Alpha Pokémon** and **herd** spawns for Gen 7, including Alolan forms: 41 Alpha spawn files (41 different Alpha Pokémon) and 29 herd spawn files.
- Herds only contain Gen 7 Pokémon and Alolan forms. Family members from other generations are left out.
- Combining packs? Herds that mix generations go back to Cobblemon's originals when all their generations are installed. For example, Gen 1 + Gen 2 brings back the Crobat-led Zubat herds.

### Spawn changes
- All Gen 7 and Alolan-form spawn rules now match Cobblemon 1.8.2: rebalanced spawn weights and biomes, plus the new Poké Snack-only spawns.
- **Salazzle** now only appears leading Salandit herds (and as an Alpha) instead of spawning on its own, matching Cobblemon 1.8.

### Habitat structures
- The new Habitat structures from Cobblemon 1.8 now only spawn Gen 7 Pokémon and Alolan forms (or Pokémon from every generation you've installed, when combining packs).
- Habitats replace normal spawning right around them, so a habitat with none of your generations' Pokémon won't spawn anything nearby. 23 of the 51 habitat types spawn nothing with only this pack installed.
- Some Pokémon spawn in habitats in both their regular and a regional form (for example, Vulpix in Wildfire Scars and Alolan Vulpix in Snowy Grottos), and Cobblemon doesn't let datapacks tell the two apart there. So no other generation's form slips in, habitats only spawn these when both generations are installed: Cubone, Diglett, Dugtrio, Marowak, Meowth, Ninetales, Persian, Raticate, Rattata, Sandshrew, Sandslash, Vulpix (with Gen 1). They still spawn normally outside habitats.
